About me

A developer who likes turning ideas into things people can use.

I'm a BS Computer Science graduate with a focus on web and mobile development. I build responsive websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and Android apps with Kotlin and Firebase.

During my studies I also worked with machine learning. For my final year project I built an AI-based eye disease prediction app using a MobileNet model.

Right now I am building my freelance portfolio and I am open to small business websites, restaurant sites, landing pages and app projects. I design for phones first, because that is where most of your customers will see your work.

    Every layout is designed for the small screen first, then expanded for larger ones.

  • Fast and light

  • Clear communication
